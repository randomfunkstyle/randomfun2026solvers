Split
=====

Source: https://icfpcontest2026.com/split
Retrieved: 2026-07-25

The official instruction glyph is Y.

- Y replaces the entering little man with two copies. They are born on the
  cells to his left and right, relative to his incoming heading, and each faces
  away from Y.
- Both copies retain A, B, and BP.
- The copies execute their birth cells and move on the tick after their birth.
- The right copy takes the parent's creation-order position. The left copy is
  newest and acts after every previously existing little man.
- Splitting is unconditional. A birth into a wall is fatal.
- A birth onto another little man kills both without a fatal error.
- Same-cell movement collisions and head-on swaps kill both without a fatal
  error. Two copies spawned onto the same cell also die.
- At most 65,536 little men may be live.
