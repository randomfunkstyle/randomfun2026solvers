Limits and errors
Every error is

401 unauthorized
 — missing or invalid key.
403 forbidden
 — the problem is practice-only.
404 not_found
 — no such problem, or it isn't released.

 — 5 of your submissions may be waiting to run at once. Wait for one to finish.
